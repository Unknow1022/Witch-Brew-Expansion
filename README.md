# 🃏 Witcher Brew Expansion (v3.0.0)

A massive content expansion for **Balatro** (Steamodded / Lovely) featuring the **Battle of Gods** post-game mode, 57+ unique Jokers, 17 Boss Blinds with Supreme Showdowns, 18 Potions & Brews with custom FX, 16 Tags, Job Cards, 5 Decks, and deep mod integrations.

> **Notice**: Early versions of this mod (which are no longer available) originated as an experiment with artificial intelligence. The mod has since been completely rewritten, refined, and handcrafted into a fully polished expansion.

---

## 📦 Requirements & Installation
1. Install **[Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods)** (v1.0.0+) and **[Lovely](https://github.com/ethangreen-dev/lovely-injector)** (v0.7.1+).
2. Place `Witcher Brew Expansion` into your Balatro `Mods` folder (`%AppData%/Balatro/Mods`).
3. *(Optional)* **CardSleeves** and **JokerDisplay** for enhanced features and UI integration.

## ⚔️ Game Modes: Battle of Gods
A dedicated post-Ante 8 challenge mode designed for extended, deep-end runs without hitting rapid exponential score walls (`naneinf`).

* **Access**: Available as a secondary option alongside the Endless mode button upon completing Ante 8.
* **Requirements**: Toggled via Mod Settings tab (`Battle of Gods: ON`) and requires the **[Amulet](https://github.com/)** mod installed.
* **Blind Progression**:
  * Small and Big Blinds are replaced by standard **Boss Blinds**.
  * The final Blind of each Ante is replaced by a **Supreme Showdown Boss Blind**.
  * **12 Dedicated Fused Boss Blinds (Ante 12+)**: Small and Big Blinds feature handcrafted hybrid bosses with combined textures and afflictions:
    * *The Obelisk* (Needle + Pillar): Play only 1 hand; cards played previously this Ante are debuffed.
    * *The Minotaur* (Ox + Hook): Discards 2 random cards per hand; sets money to $0 if most played hand is played.
    * *The Fortress* (Wall + Flint): Extra large blind (3X target); Base Chips and Mult are halved.
    * *The Mind Flayer* (Psychic + Arm): Must play 5 cards; decreases level of played poker hand.
    * *The Leviathan* (Water + Fish): Start with 0 discards; cards drawn face down after each hand played.
    * *The Iron Maiden* (Manacle + Tooth): -1 Hand Size; lose $1 per card played.
    * *The Cyclops* (Eye + Mouth): Only 1 hand type allowed this round; repeat hands are debuffed.
    * *The Thorn Crown* (Mark + Plant): All Face cards are drawn face down and debuffed.
    * *The Ouroboros* (Wheel + Serpent): Always draw 3 cards after play or discard; 1 in 7 cards drawn face down.
    * *The Nightshade* (House + Goad): First hand drawn face down; all Spades are debuffed.
    * *The Black Diamond* (Window + Club): All Clubs and Diamonds are debuffed.
    * *The Blood Moon* (Head + Goad): All Hearts and Spades are debuffed.
* **Exclusive Supreme Showdowns**:
  * **Chronos**: Must defeat the blind in 2 hands or fewer.
  * **Ares**: Scored cards rank 7 or lower lose $1 each.
  * **Athena**: Cannot play the same poker hand type twice in a row.
  * **Hades**: Rental fees and score requirements surge under pressure.
  * **Zeus**: Unpredictable lightning strikes and hand penalties.
* **Boss Possession (Possessed Sticker)**:
  * Defeating a Boss offers the chance to possess a Joker with the fallen boss's spirit.
  * Displays the custom **Possessed Sticker** and inverts the boss's curse into an overwhelming player blessing (e.g. Needle grants **X10 Mult**, Flint grants **X2 Mult** per scoring card, Pillar grants **+150 Chips and +20 Mult** per played card).
* **Mini-Boss Familiars**:
  * A dedicated companion slot directly beneath the Consumables area (`G.consumeables`), visible exclusively during Battle of Gods.
  * Holds 1 active Mini-Boss pet with an interactive replacement modal upon discovering a new one:
    * *Baby Needle*: +100 Chips and +15 Mult when winning in 1 hand.
    * *Baby Pillar*: Shields all cards in hand from Boss debuffs.
    * *Baby Serpent*: Discarding immediately draws +1 extra card from deck.
    * *Baby Flint*: Scored cards permanently gain +5 Chips and +2 Mult.
    * *Baby Hook*: Discards cannot drop below 1; grants 1 free shop reroll.
    * *Baby Eye*: Playing repeat poker hands triggers X1.5 Mult.
* **Direct God Combat & Dynamic Encounters**:
  * **Turn-Based Counter-Attacks**: Boss Blinds retaliate with Divine Wrath (Divine Zap, Intimidation Roar, Aegis Pulse) if they survive a played hand.
  * **Chronos Paradox (Rewind Machine)**: A golden interactive HUD button usable once per Ante to undo a misplay while locking in 50% of scored chips as Temporal Echo.
  * **Dimensional Glitch Tears**: Cosmic rifts that corrupt cards with unpredictable chromatic glitches and wild multipliers (X0.5 to X8.0 Mult).
* **Divine Perks & Rewards**:
  * **Divine Ward**: The first boss blind reroll in each Ante is guaranteed free.
  * **Overkill to Divine Grace**: Excess score above target converts into Divine Grace currency.
  * **Thanatos's Hourglass**: Conquering a Showdown cleanses all Perishable timers and purges Rental debuffs.
  * **Apotheosis (Ante 20+)**: Your primary Joker ascends to Deity status (+X2 Mult and full boss debuff immunity).
  * **Hand Level Transcendence**: Hands leveled above Lv 20 trigger an Exalted X1.25 Multiplier.
* **Shop Perks & Pantheon Expansions**:
  * Automatically redeems **Taster** and **Critic** vouchers to eliminate Common Jokers.
  * **Pantheon Pack ($25)**: Mythic booster offering 3 Secret or Legendary Jokers.
  * **Pantheon Vouchers**: **Ambrosia** (+1 Consumable slot) and **Nectar** (Shop rerolls shave up to 30% off Boss target scores).
  * Enables **Legendary** and **Secret Jokers** in standard shop slots.
* **Hall of Gods & Aesthetics**: High-contrast Black & White monochrome vortex background with persistent Titan Slayer boss defeat tracking.

## 📜 Changelog

### v3.0.0
* **New Game Mode - Battle of Gods**: Introduced a dedicated post-Ante 8 mode accessible alongside Endless when the Amulet mod is present.
  * Replaces Small and Big Blinds with regular Boss Blinds, and Boss Blinds with Showdown Boss Blinds.
  * Rebalanced blind score scaling to sustain long runs and prevent rapid `naneinf` soft-locks.
  * Automatically grants and redeems Taster and Critic vouchers upon starting the mode.
  * Enables Legendary and Secret Jokers to spawn naturally in shop offerings.
  * Implemented custom high-contrast Black & White monochrome vortex background.
* **Mod Configuration**: Added in-game toggle in the Settings tab to configure Battle of Gods mode.
* **UI & Versioning**: Synchronized mod manifest, core definitions, and settings interface to v3.0.0.

### v2.1.0
* **The Doppelgänger Rework**: Reworked showdown boss blind mechanics. The Doppelgänger now possesses 1 of your Jokers; whenever that Joker activates, both Chips and Mult are divided by 4 (÷4 Chips & Mult penalty).
* **Blueprint & Brainstorm Overhaul**: Fixed issue where copying card-spawning Jokers (e.g. Certificate, DNA, Cartomancer, Marble, Mascarada, Balance) caused duplicate triggers, queue desyncs, and consumable slot overflow.
* **Simulation Safety**: `Falta de Lectura` simulation context now excludes copier Jokers and prevents queuing side-effect spawn events.
* **Consumable Buffer Protection**: Implemented atomic `consumeable_buffer` tracking across all card-granting Jokers (`Headhunter`, `Balance`, `Mascarada`, `Alchemist`, `Miner`).
* **Custom Blind Lighting & Showdown Stability**: Showdown Blinds (`The Void`, `The Pincer`, `The Doppelgänger`) and Boss Blinds reliably preserve custom ambient color palettes and will not be overwritten by vanilla Showdown Blue/Red backgrounds.
* **Full English Localization**: Translated remaining boss texts and variables (including `The Doppelgänger`).
* **Steamodded & Lovely Requirements**: Updated dependency declarations to require Steamodded `>=26.829.0` and Lovely; removed legacy BMM metadata (`meta.json`).
